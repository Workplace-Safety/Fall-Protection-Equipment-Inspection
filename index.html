OSHA Requirement (29 CFR 1926.501-503): Fall protection required for work at 6+ ft. Personal fall arrest systems must be inspected before each use. Defective equipment must be removed from service immediately.
Inspection #:

1. Full-Body Harness Inspection

Webbing free of cuts, tears, abrasions, or fraying
No burns, chemical damage, or discoloration in webbing
D-ring intact, no cracks, bending, or distortion
Buckles function properly, no sharp edges or deformation
Stitching intact, no loose or broken threads
Identification tag present and legible (manufacturer, date, model)

2. Lanyard/Shock Absorber Inspection

Lanyard webbing/rope free from damage or wear
Snap hooks operate smoothly, locking mechanism works properly
Shock absorber pack intact (not deployed or damaged)
No corrosion, pitting, or damage to metal components

3. Self-Retracting Lifeline (SRL) - If Applicable

SRL cable/webbing retracts smoothly without binding
Housing intact, no cracks or damage
Swivel operates freely

4. Anchorage Points (If Fixed/Installed)

Anchor capable of supporting 5,000 lbs per person attached
Anchorage securely attached to structure
Anchorage positioned to prevent swing fall hazards
⚠ CRITICAL: Equipment that has been subjected to fall arrest (fall occurred) must be immediately removed from service and destroyed or sent for manufacturer inspection.
